What We Build

Four disciplines, one program.

Most electronic products fail at the seams between teams. We keep power, interface, connectivity, and hardware under one roof so the tradeoffs get made together.

Energy Conversion01

Power electronics & motor control

Sensorless DTFC vector control and power-stage design that hold efficiency from low to high speed while cutting BOM cost.

  • Sensorless torque & flux control (DTFC)
  • Inverter & power-stage design
  • Efficiency, thermal & EMI optimization
HMI02

Human-machine interfaces

Firmware and interaction design for the panel, display, or app the end user actually touches — built to survive production, not just a demo.

  • Touch, button & rotary control firmware
  • Display & graphics driver integration
  • UX flows tuned for industrial & appliance use
Connectivity & IoT03

Wireless & cloud connectivity

Devices that report, update, and get diagnosed remotely — Wi-Fi, BLE, and cellular links wired into a cloud telemetry pipeline.

  • Wi-Fi / BLE / cellular module integration
  • Cloud telemetry & remote diagnostics
  • Secure OTA firmware update pipelines
Hardware Design04

Schematic, PCB & certification

The physical layer everything above runs on — designed for manufacturability and taken through the regulatory approvals that let it ship.

  • Schematic capture & PCB layout
  • DFM / DFT for volume manufacturing
  • Regulatory & safety certification support
